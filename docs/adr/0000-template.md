# ADR NNNN — Title in sentence case

- **Status**: proposed
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: who made the call
- **Related**: issue, spec or PR links

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints, and what happens if nothing is decided.

## Decision

What we will do, stated in full sentences. Name the files, interfaces or settings it changes.

## Consequences

### Positive

- …

### Negative

- …

## Alternatives considered

- **Option** — why it was not chosen.

## Supersedes / Superseded by

- Supersedes: none
- Superseded by: none
