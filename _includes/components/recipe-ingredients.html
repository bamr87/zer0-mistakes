Params: ingredients (array, optional, default: page.ingredients) heading (string, optional, default: "Ingredients") heading_level (string, optional, default: "h2") id_prefix (string, optional, default: page.slug) — checkbox ids checklist (bool, optional, default: true) — false renders a plain list with no checkboxes Depends on: components/recipe-qty.html, _data/ingredient_densities.yml, assets/js/recipe-scaler.js, _sass/components/_recipe.scss Notes: Accepts a flat list of items or a list of {group, items} groups, and tolerates the two mixed. Densities resolve as item.grams_per_cup → _data/ingredient_densities[slug] → none; without one, a volume can never be converted to a weight. Each line carries itemprop="recipeIngredient" for schema.org.
