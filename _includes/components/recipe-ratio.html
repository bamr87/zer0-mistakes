{% comment %} Feature: ZER0-084 {% endcomment %} {%- comment -%} Component: recipe-ratio Path: _includes/components/recipe-ratio.html Purpose: The formula behind the recipe — every ingredient reduced to a weight and a percentage of one reference ingredient. This is the baker's-percentage table bread, pastry and sauce recipes are really built on, and it is what lets a reader rebuild the recipe at any size instead of only at multiples of the printed one. Params: recipe (doc, optional, default: page) basis (string, optional, default: page.ratio_basis) — the ingredient that counts as 100%; matched as a substring, so "flour" covers "bread flour" + "whole-wheat flour" heading (string, optional, default: "Ratio") heading_level (string, optional, default: "h2") Depends on: components/recipe-grams.html, components/recipe-qty.html, _data/ingredient_densities.yml, _sass/components/_recipe.scss Notes: Percentages are computed at BUILD time, so the table is right with JavaScript disabled and never drifts from the ingredient list. They are also scale-invariant — only the weight column moves when the reader rescales. Ingredients that cannot be resolved to a weight (a clove of garlic, "1 egg", a volume with no density) are listed without a percentage rather than guessed at. Set `ratio: false` on an ingredient to leave it out, or author `baker_percent:` to pin a value. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _rr_r = include.recipe | default: page -%} {%- assign _rr_basis =
